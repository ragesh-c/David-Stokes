The Author

A historian who came home to fiction

David Stokes studied history at Oxford and spent thirty years in academia before returning to the stories he had carried since university. His Anglo-Saxon novels recover the women the chronicles erased and place them at the centre of the epic story of how England was made. From the warring kingdoms of the 6th century to the fall before 1066, his fiction dramatises the pivotal moments history tried to forget.
