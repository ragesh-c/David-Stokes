Sermon of the Wolf

Foreshore Books  ·  The final chapter of the Anglo-Saxon Arc

England, 1002. A young Norman duchess is crowned queen of a kingdom on its knees — Viking fleets on every coast, a weak king on the throne, treachery in every council chamber. Emma of Normandy will spend the next half-century outmanoeuvring them all: inept kings, scheming earls, Viking conquerors, even her own sons. Twice queen. Mother to three kings. The woman who unknowingly sowed the seed of 1066 — and could not stop what was coming.
