The Singing Bowl

A new novel in support of COP26

2021 · All profits to Zero Carbon Guildford

Buy on Amazon UK

The Story

On Thera — a sister planet to Earth, where the dinosaurs were never wiped out — three teenagers discover that their world is in crisis. The same forces that are destroying Earth's climate are at work here. But on Thera, the consequences are already visible. And the teenagers are the only ones who understand what is coming.

Written to coincide with COP26, The Singing Bowl is a novel about the climate emergency aimed at readers of all ages. All profits from sales go to Zero Carbon Guildford. It is a different kind of book from David Stokes's historical fiction — lighter in register, urgent in purpose.
